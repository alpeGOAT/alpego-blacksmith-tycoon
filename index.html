WELCOME TO THE WORKSHOP

From sparks to gold.

You start with 100 gold. Run your weapon shop for 30 days and finish with as much cash as you can.

  • Choose quantities and prices for swords, axes, spears, and daggers each day. New shops start with forge level 0: 20 weapons total per day. Buy level 1 for 40 or level 2 for 60 weapons per day.
  • Market prices run from swords (30 gold), axes (22), spears (16), to daggers (10). Base crafting costs are 14, 10, 7, and 4 gold respectively. Better anvil levels 1, 2, and 3 reduce every crafting cost by 1, 2, and 3 gold respectively. At level 3, costs are 11, 7, 4, and 1 gold.
  • Buy workshop upgrades between days: Better anvil has three levels at 50 gold each (150 gold total); Improved forge starts at level 0, with levels 1 and 2 costing 75 gold each. Precision vise (65) and Improved advertising (50) can each be bought once. The vise boosts demand as if each price were 2 gold lower. Improved advertising makes paid ads count as 50% more spending before diminishing returns. Purchases reduce cash immediately and appear under Workshop investments; total profit includes their cost. You cannot buy upgrades while the shop is open or after day 30.
  • Each weapon has its own buyers and inventory. Higher prices attract fewer buyers; rarer expensive weapons have lower base demand.
  • Advertising promotes the weapons you offer, with diminishing returns. Total demand is capped at 60 weapons per day across your shop. All crafting and ads must fit your cash upfront.
  • Unsold stock carries over by weapon. Set crafting to zero to sell stock you already own. No rent or other fees.
  • Customer satisfaction starts at 70/100. Only weapons you have available to sell count toward satisfaction. Prices below market value help and high prices hurt. Filling all demand adds 4 points, while shortages can cost up to 4. Offering no weapons costs 4 points. Price effects range from −6 to +6 points. The score stays between 0 and 100. The evening report explains every change. The opening satisfaction score affects that day’s demand: 0 gives −30%, 50 is neutral, and 100 gives +30% (70 starts at +12%). The evening score applies to the next day, alongside prices, advertising and market conditions. Actual sales remain limited by available stock. Final score is still remaining cash.
  • The game ends after day 30, or earlier if you have no stock and cannot afford to craft any weapon. Your final score is your remaining gold.

Your game saves automatically in this browser. If another tab changes the save, you’ll see the latest state before you can continue.
