WELCOME TO THE WORKSHOP

From sparks to gold.

You start with 100 gold. Run your weapon shop for 30 days and finish with as much cash as you can.

  • Choose quantities and prices for swords, axes, spears, and daggers each day. Craft up to 40 weapons in total.
  • Market prices run from swords (30 gold), axes (22), spears (16), to daggers (10). Crafting costs are 14, 10, 7, and 4 gold respectively.
  • Each weapon has its own buyers and inventory. Higher prices attract fewer buyers; rarer expensive weapons have lower base demand.
  • Advertising promotes the whole shop with diminishing returns. All crafting and ads must fit your cash upfront.
  • Unsold stock carries over by weapon. Set crafting to zero to sell stock you already own. No rent or other fees.
  • The game ends after day 30. Final score is cash; remaining stock is not liquidated.

Your game saves automatically in this browser when storage is available.
